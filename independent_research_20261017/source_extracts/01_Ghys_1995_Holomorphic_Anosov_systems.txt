1. Introduction 
The group of holomorphic diffeomorphisms of a compact complex manifold is a 
finite dimensional Lie group and one could expect a rather complete description 
of the dynamical behaviour of such diffeomorphisms, at least in low dimensions. 
For instance, holomorphic diffeomorphisms of compact K/ihler manifolds, pre- 
serving the cohomology class of the K~ihler form, have very simple dynamics, 
with zero topological entropy [Fu], [Sn]. A theorem of F. Enriques describes 
alyebraic surfaces for which the group of holomorphic diffeomorphisms has 
infinitely many connected components [En], [Ro]. 
However, very interesting examples do exist and deserve attention. Some 
K3-surfaces have an infinite discrete group of diffeomorphisms whose dyna-

a holomorphic diffeomorphism,4 of the complex torus ~n/A. If the spectrum
